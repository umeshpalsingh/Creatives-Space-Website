Meet Creatives Space

Outdoor spaces crafted like architecture — and built as if they were our own.

20+Years

Led by owners Kevin & Liliane Fronterhouse, we design, build and personally oversee every project — so the vision you approve is exactly what you get.

Kevin & LilianeOwners · Fronterhouse family

Want to bring color to your yard, finally build the outdoor kitchen you've always imagined, or turn a muddy slope into a place to gather?

Creatives Space is the family-owned and operated landscaping company Northwest Arkansas homeowners call when they want it done right. For more than 20 years we've specialized in outdoor living spaces, landscape design, hardscaping and irrigation — balancing professionalism and practicality with aesthetics and inspiration.

What we create

Every element of the outdoor estate.

One accountable team for design, construction and finishing — from the drainage beneath your lawn to the fire you gather around.

  • Planting plans, sod installation, mulching, artificial turf, flower beds, shrubs and trees — composed to look established from day one and mature beautifully for years.

  • Paths, steps, walkways, rock beds and natural stonework that give your landscape structure, flow and year-round character — balancing the green with lasting form.
