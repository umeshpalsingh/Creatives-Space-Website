Good to know

Common questions.

Don't see your question here? Give us a call — we're happy to talk through your project.

Call 479-530-4767
Which areas do you serve?

We work throughout Northwest Arkansas, including Bella Vista, Bentonville, Rogers, Lowell, Springdale, Tontitown, Fayetteville, West Fork and the surrounding communities.

Do you handle both design and construction?

Yes. We design, install and manage your project from start to finish. Our outdoor living space builder is a licensed contractor, so we can oversee construction work in addition to your landscaping.

How do I get started?

Call or text 479-530-4767, or send the form above. We'll set up a time to visit your property, listen to your ideas and talk through priorities, timing and budget.

Will I know the cost before work begins?

Yes. After your consultation you'll receive a clear estimate for your project before any work starts, and we'll communicate openly if anything needs to change along the way.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

Everything from landscape design, sod and planting to patios, decks, outdoor kitchens and fireplaces, retaining walls, irrigation, drainage solutions and backhoe work. See our services for details.
