Outdoor living spaces

A breath of fresh air in your backyard.

If you want to make the most of your backyard, a custom outdoor living space is a fantastic project.

Our outdoor living space builder is a licensed contractor, so we can oversee all of your construction work in addition to your landscaping. And to create a comfortable, shaded spot to relax, we can also install a cover over your new patio.

Hardscape design & installation

Hardscaping balances the green space in your yard and creates a more complex, eye-catching design. We'll outline all your ideas together, then build features that let you dictate the flow and structure of your yard.
